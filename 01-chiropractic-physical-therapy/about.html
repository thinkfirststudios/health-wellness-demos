About the clinic

A Neighborhood Clinic for Better Movement

Cornerpost opened as a small chiropractic practice and grew into a joint chiropractic and physical therapy clinic, because patients kept asking for both under one roof.

2014Year the clinic openedSample

Our story

Care That Explains Itself

We believe people do better when they understand what's going on. So every first visit ends with a plain-language explanation and a written plan — and every follow-up starts by asking how the plan is working for you.

Our chiropractors and physical therapists share one treatment room floor, one record and a weekly case review. If your plan would work better with the other discipline, you'll hear it from us first.

  • Licensed chiropractors and physical therapists
  • Weekly joint case reviews
  • Adults only, 18 and over
