Your first visit

What Happens at Your First Appointment

Sixty unhurried minutes: your history, a movement exam, a clear explanation and a plan you agree with. Here's how it runs.

Book a first visit

Your First Hour, Step by Step

Every first visit follows the same shape, whether you're seeing a chiropractor, a physical therapist or both.

  1. Step 1

    Arrival & check-in

    Arrive ten minutes early. The front desk confirms your details and insurance — you'll already have completed the intake form online.

    About 10 minutes
  2. Step 2

    Your history

    Your clinician asks what brought you in, how it affects your day and what you'd like to get back to. You can bring someone with you.

    About 15 minutes
  3. Step 3

    Movement examination

    We look at how you move: range of motion, strength, balance and specific tests for the area involved. Wear comfortable clothing you can move in.

    About 20 minutes
  4. Step 4

    Your plan & first treatment

    We explain what we found in plain language, agree a plan with you, and usually begin treatment and your first home exercises the same day.

    About 15 minutes

What to bring

  • Photo ID and your insurance card
  • A list of any operations in the last two years
  • Comfortable clothing, such as shorts or leggings
  • Glasses if you need them to read

Questions

First-visit Questions, Answered

Will I be treated on the first visit?

Usually, yes. If anything in your assessment suggests you need a medical opinion first, we'll explain why and wait.

Do I need a referral?

Most plans don't require one for chiropractic or physical therapy. We'll check your plan before you arrive.

Will I need to undress?

Rarely. Shorts and a T-shirt or tank top let us see how you move. Gowns are available if you prefer.

How many visits will I need?

It depends on you. We'll suggest a first block of visits, review progress, and never ask you to pre-pay for a long package.

Can I bring someone with me?

Of course. A friend or family member is welcome to join you in the treatment room.

What does it cost?

Self-pay first visits are listed on our insurance & fees page. With insurance, we'll tell you your expected share before you come in.
