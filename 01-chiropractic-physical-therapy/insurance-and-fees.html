Insurance

Plans We Work With

We're in network with most major PPO plans and bill them directly. If we're out of network for your plan, we'll give you a superbill to claim back yourself.

  • Most PPO plans
  • Medicare Part B (physical therapy)
  • Workers' compensation
  • Auto insurance claims
  • HSA & FSA cards
  • Superbills for out-of-network plans

We can't tell you exactly what your plan covers until we check it. Coverage, co-pays and visit limits vary by plan.

Paying for care

Billing, Simply Explained

How to pay

Card, HSA/FSA card or cash, at the end of each visit. We don't ask you to pre-pay for blocks of visits.

Estimates before you start

With insurance, we'll tell you your expected share before your first visit, in writing if you like.

Cancellations

Please give 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations and missed visits may carry a fee, waived for emergencies.
