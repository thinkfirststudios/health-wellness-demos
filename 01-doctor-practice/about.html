What it is

One practice, one building

Braeburn Family Medicine is an independent primary care practice. We see adults, and children from age two. We work with most major commercial plans, Medicare, two Medicaid managed-care plans and people paying for themselves.

The practice is owned by its physicians, and it belongs to no hospital or health system. Decisions about appointment lengths and who we take on are made here, at the practice.

Each week one person at the front desk checks the panel status with each provider and updates this site. That is why you will see a date next to every status.

Languages

Spanish and interpreting

English and Spanish are spoken here every day. If you prefer Spanish, tell us when you call and we will make sure someone is with you.

For any other language we use a phone interpreter, at no cost to you. Ask when you book and we will arrange it for your appointment.

The building

Suite 2, on the ground floor

You come in from Candler Street to the front desk. The four rooms run along one corridor, and the restroom is on the same floor.
