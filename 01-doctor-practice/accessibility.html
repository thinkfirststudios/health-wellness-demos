Getting in

Arriving and the door

Entry. There is step-free entry from Candler Street, and there are no steps inside.

Parking. There are two accessible bays, left of the entrance. They are not reserved by name, and the path from them to the door is level.

The door. The front door is a manual double door and it does not open automatically. If you cannot manage it, call (555) 555-0142 from outside, or press the bell at the right of the door, and someone will come and open it.

Inside

Rooms, restroom and hearing

Examination tables. Rooms 1 and 2 have height-adjustable tables. Rooms 3 and 4 do not: their tables are fixed. If you need the lower table, tell us when you book. Priya Raman and Dr Vlček can see you in Room 1 or 2 when you ask ahead.

Restroom. There is an accessible restroom on the same floor as the rooms.

Hearing. There is a hearing loop at reception. There is no loop in the examination rooms. If you need one, ask us about other ways to communicate.

Your appointment

Asking for what you need

Longer appointments. Ask when you book and we will give you a double slot, at no extra charge for the additional time.

Someone with you. You are welcome to bring a family member, a caregiver or a support animal.

Language. English and Spanish are spoken every day. A phone interpreter is available for anything else, at no cost.

Something we have not mentioned? Please call and ask. If this page gets something wrong, tell us and we will correct it.
