When you could actually be seen

The last appointment is the latest time a visit can start. A new-patient visit needs 40 minutes, so it has to start at least 40 minutes before we close.

We hold a few same-week slots each weekday for something new. They go in the order people call. Priya Raman sees most of them.

(555) 555-0142

Holidays. We close on federal holidays, and at 12:00 pm on the day before Thanksgiving and the day before Christmas. Closures are posted here two weeks ahead.

After we close. Call the main number and follow the recording to reach the on-call provider. They can advise on whether something can wait until morning. They cannot book appointments, refill prescriptions or give results by phone. Those wait until we open.

In an emergency, call 911. We do not provide emergency care.
