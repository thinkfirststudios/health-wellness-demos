Billing

How billing works

If you use a plan

We send the claim to your plan. Your plan decides what it pays and what you owe, and it writes to you. We then send one statement for any balance, usually within 30 days. Copays are collected at the desk.

If you pay yourself

You pay the price in the table at the end of the visit, by card or cash. Tell us before the visit starts so we do not send a claim.

What is billed separately

Lab work, imaging and anything sent out are billed by whoever does them, not by us. Vaccines are charged in addition to the visit. Ask us before the visit if you want the vaccine price.

If you cannot pay

Tell the front desk or call us. We can split a bill into up to six monthly payments, with no interest and no fee. We do not send a bill to collections while a payment plan is in place, and we will not turn you away from a booked visit over a balance without talking to you first.

Paying a bill

Your statement explains how to pay by phone or at the desk. We do not take card details on this website.
