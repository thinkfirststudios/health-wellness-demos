Registering

What happens when you register

Start by choosing a provider who is accepting new patients.

  1. Call us or use the form. Tell us who you would like to see.
  2. We call you back for 10 minutes to check the practice fits: your plan, your provider, and anything we do not do.
  3. We request your records from your previous practice. You sign one release.
  4. You have a 40-minute first appointment with your provider.
  5. After that you see the same person. If they are away, the roster shows who is free.

Start with the booking form

What to bring

For your first appointment

  • A photo ID
  • Your insurance card, if you have one
  • Your current medicines, or a list of them
  • Your previous practice's name and phone number
  • For a child: any school forms and their vaccination record
Records

How your records move

One release, signed once.

We need the name of your previous practice and one signed release, which we give you at the call-back. We then request your records for you. Most arrive within 10 working days.

You do not have to wait for them to be seen. If they have not arrived by your first appointment, we go ahead, and your provider will ask you more questions.

If they never arrive: we ask twice more over 30 days, then tell you. Some practices close or lose files. We then start your record from what you tell us and from any papers you have, and you can ask your pharmacy for a list of your prescriptions.

The first appointment

What it is for

Your first appointment is 40 minutes. It is for your provider to get to know you, go through your history and agree what happens next. It is in person, not by video. Please arrive 10 minutes early for the paperwork.

Patient portal

The patient portal, in words

A separate secure site, not this one.

After your first appointment we send you a link by text or email to set up the portal. It is a separate secure site, run for us by a records company. Use it for messages to your provider, test results and repeat prescription requests.

It is not for anything urgent, and it is not checked outside our hours. You will never sign in on this website, and we will never ask for your password.
