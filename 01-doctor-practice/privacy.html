What we collect here

Last reviewed 4 November.

The booking form

The form asks for your name, how to reach you, which provider you would like, in person or video, and what the request is about. That is all it collects. It goes to our front desk, who use it to call you back and then delete the message.

The form is not a secure channel, which is why it asks you not to describe symptoms or history.

No analytics, no tracking

This site has no analytics, no advertising or social tracking, and no chat tool. It uses no cookies. Its fonts and images are served from this site, so your visit is not shared with another company.

The patient portal

The portal is a separate site with its own sign-in and its own privacy terms. Nothing you do on this website is passed to it.

Your health records

This page covers this website only. Your health records have their own notice, which we give you when you register.

Questions
