How they work

How each provider likes to work

Choose a person, not just a time.

Dr Adaeze Okonjo, MD

Dr Okonjo works in Room 1. She likes a first visit to end with a written list of what happens next, and she books follow-ups before you leave. She shares pregnancy care with an obstetric practice, and she sees people with diabetes or high blood pressure for regular follow-up.

Dr Martin Hollis, MD

Dr Hollis works in Room 2. He mostly sees adults over 50 and people coming home from a hospital stay, and he allows extra time for those visits. His panel is full. He takes new patients from the waitlist as places open, in the order people joined.

Priya Raman, FNP

Priya Raman works in Room 3. She is a family nurse practitioner, not a physician. She sees most short-notice visits, and she runs contraception, sexual health and travel vaccination appointments.

Dr Tomas Vlček, DO

Dr Vlček works in Room 4. He is taking new patients who are children, from age two. Parents registering at the same time can join Dr Okonjo or Priya Raman, and we book the family together when we can.
