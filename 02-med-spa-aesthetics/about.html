About the clinic

Small, physician-led and unhurried

Why Kilnwood exists

Kilnwood opened as a four-room clinic for people who wanted aesthetic treatment without a sales pitch. The idea was simple: give every client a proper consultation, write the plan down, and price it clearly before anything happens.

We keep the menu focused on treatments our team performs often and well — clinical facials, peels, microneedling, injectables, laser hair removal and a short list of body treatments. If you would be better served elsewhere, we will say so and suggest where.

How treatments are supervised

Licensed people, clear responsibilities

Medical oversight

A physician medical director approves every medical protocol and reviews injectable plans. Medical treatments are performed by a physician or a registered nurse.

Skin and laser

Facials, peels and microneedling are carried out by licensed aestheticians; laser by technicians certified in laser safety, under written protocols.

What we will not do

No treatment is sold on the day of a first visit without a consultation, and we do not treat anyone under 18.

The team

Roles, not names — every practitioner is licensed

Medical Director

A board-certified physician who oversees every medical protocol, signs off injectable plans and is on call during clinic hours.

Nurse Injector, RN

A registered nurse who carries out consultations and injectable treatments under the medical director's supervision.

Licensed Aesthetician

Leads facials, peels and microneedling, and writes the home-care plan you leave with.

Laser Technician

Certified in laser safety; carries out patch tests and laser hair removal courses.
