About

A medical practice that happens to be about appearance

Kilnwood is an independent practice in Ellery Mills. Everything it does is a medical procedure or sits beside one, and it is run that way.

  • Medical director: a physician, on site every Tuesday and Thursday and on call on every other treatment day.
  • Practitioners: a nurse practitioner, registered nurses and a licensed aesthetician. Every current state license hangs at reception.
  • Who does what: set out against every treatment in the treatment table.

The medical director

The medical director is a physician who sets every protocol here, reviews every recovery figure on this site and decides what each role may do.

They are on site every Tuesday and Thursday and on call on every other treatment day. They personally perform laser resurfacing, biostimulant injections and leg vein injections.

What each person may do differs from state to state, so our delegation follows this state’s rules, in writing, and you can ask to see it.

Who does what here

  • Medical director

    A physician. Sets every protocol, supervises delegated work, performs laser resurfacing, biostimulant and vein injections, and is on site every Tuesday and Thursday.

  • Nurse practitioner

    Carries out consultations and prescribes. Performs neuromodulator and filler injections, radiofrequency microneedling and medium-depth peels.

  • Registered nurse

    Works by delegation from the medical director. Performs laser hair reduction, intense pulsed light, microneedling and cooling body contouring.

  • Licensed aesthetician

    Performs light chemical peels and does not inject or operate lasers here.

What you will not find on this site

Photographs of patients

One person’s face, on one good day, in one kind of light, tells you very little about yours. We show you rooms and recovery figures, and we talk through your own case in person.

Patient reviews

A review of a medical procedure is one person’s experience, and it cannot tell you what yours will be. Ask us anything at the consultation instead.

Product and device names

We describe everything by what it is. If you want to know exactly what would be used on you, ask, and you will get a straight answer.

Offers and countdowns

Nobody should decide on a medical procedure against a clock, so we do not run seasonal deals or deadlines.
