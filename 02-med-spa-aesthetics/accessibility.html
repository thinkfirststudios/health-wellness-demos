Accessibility

Getting here, getting in, using this site

The building

  • A step-free entrance on the side path, with a level threshold and a door that opens automatically.
  • Reception, all four treatment rooms and the accessible restroom are on the ground floor.
  • Two accessible parking spaces are closest to the side entrance.
  • Treatment beds are height-adjustable. Tell us if you would like help getting on or off the bed.
  • Service animals are welcome.

During your visit

  • We can book a longer appointment if you would like more time to talk or to rest between steps.
  • You are welcome to bring someone with you to your consultation.
  • Aftercare instructions are available in large print, or by email so you can use your own reading tools.
  • If you would like lights dimmed or music off during a treatment, just ask.

This website

We aim for this site to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. It works with a keyboard, has a skip link and visible focus outlines, uses text with strong contrast against the dark background, and respects your device's reduced-motion setting: the reviews slider will not rotate on its own when reduced motion is set, and it can be paused at any time.

All page content is visible without JavaScript. The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts or maps.

Something not working? Tell us at hello@kilnwood.example or (555) 0148-2210, and say which page and what you were trying to do. We aim to reply within two working days.
