Accessibility

Getting in, getting around, and using this site

These are the facts of the building, so you can decide for yourself before you travel. If something here does not match what you find, please tell us.

  • Medical director: a physician, on site every Tuesday and Thursday and on call on every other treatment day.
  • Practitioners: a nurse practitioner, registered nurses and a licensed aesthetician. Every current state license hangs at reception.
  • Who does what: set out against every treatment in the treatment table.

The building

Step-free entry
Yes, from Tannery Row. The door opens automatically.
Door width
36 inches at the street door and every treatment room.
Lift or stairs
Neither. Every room is on the ground floor.
Parking
Two accessible spaces in the rear lot, 30 feet from the back door.
Public transport
The Route 4 bus stops at the corner of Tannery Row.
Restroom
An accessible restroom with grab rails, beside the waiting area.
Couch height
Every treatment couch is powered and lowers to 20 inches.
Who to call ahead
The front desk, on (603) 555-0142. Tell us what you need and we will set up the room.

This website

Every recovery scale here can be read without color: each step is numbered and written out in words. Tables keep their labels on a phone, motion stops if your device asks for reduced motion, and everything can be reached by keyboard.

If any part of this site does not work for you, call (603) 555-0142 or email hello@kilnwoodclinic.example and we will give you the same information another way.
