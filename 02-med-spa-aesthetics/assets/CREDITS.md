All photographs come from Pexels under the Pexels licence, which allows free commercial use and needs no attribution. They are recorded here so their source can be traced. Each one was checked by eye on a contact sheet. None shows a watermark, a legible brand mark or product label, a child or teenager, or obvious AI artefacts. Files were cropped to the ratio of their slot. The clinical photos were also toned down slightly (brightness 0.86, saturation 0.88) to sit on the charcoal background. These IDs are used only in this template. The people shown are stock models and are never named. Team cards show role titles only.

## Fonts

All fonts are from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font Licence. They are self-hosted in `fonts/` as latin-subset woff2 files.
- Abril Fatface 400 (`abril-fatface-400.woff2`), for the wordmark and headings
- Libre Baskerville, variable 400–700 (`libre-baskerville-var.woff2`) and italic 400 (`libre-baskerville-italic.woff2`), for body text, navigation and quotes
- Pinyon Script 400 (`pinyon-script-400.woff2`), for the script accent lines
