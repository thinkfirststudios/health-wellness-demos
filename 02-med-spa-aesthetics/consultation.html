Consultation

Forty-five minutes, before anything happens

Almost everything we do starts here. You will talk to the person who would treat you, see the figures for your own case, and leave with a written quote.

  • Medical director: a physician, on site every Tuesday and Thursday and on call on every other treatment day.
  • Practitioners: a nurse practitioner, registered nurses and a licensed aesthetician. Every current state license hangs at reception.
  • Who does what: set out against every treatment in the treatment table.

What the consultation is

Is the consultation free or paid?
Paid: $75.
How long is it?
45 minutes.
Who will I see?
A nurse practitioner, or the medical director for laser resurfacing and vein injections.
Is anything done on the same day?
No injectable or energy treatment is done at a first consultation.
Is the fee credited against treatment?
Yes, in full, if you go ahead within 60 days.
Will I be sold something on this visit?
No. You leave with a written quote and the recovery figures, and we do not offer packages at this appointment.
What can’t I have without a consultation?
Everything except a repeat light peel.

What to bring

A photo ID and a list of any medicines you take. If you have had aesthetic treatment elsewhere, the dates and what was done.

Choose a time

Times for the coming week. Thursdays run into the evening, so the first days of recovery can fall on your weekend.
