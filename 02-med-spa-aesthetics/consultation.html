Book a consultation

The first step for every treatment

Forty-five minutes with a licensed practitioner, in a private room, with no obligation to book anything.

What happens at the consultation

Unhurried, private and in plain language

Your goals

We start by asking what you would like to change and why now. There is no checklist of things you ought to want, and saying you are only exploring is completely fine.

A look at your skin

Your practitioner examines your skin in good light and, with your permission, takes reference photographs that stay in your private clinical record.

Your health, in person

Health questions that affect treatment are asked in the room, by a licensed practitioner, and recorded in your clinical file. We never ask for them through this website.

A written plan

You leave with the options discussed, the number of sessions, the price and the aftercare. If the right answer is to do nothing, we will tell you that too.

Request a consultation

Tell us roughly what you are interested in and when suits you. The clinic will reply within one working day to offer a time.

Please do not include medical conditions, medications or any diagnosis in this form. Your practitioner will ask about your health in person.
