Recovery & aftercare

What to expect after your visit

General guidance only. Your practitioner will give you written aftercare for your own treatment, and that is the advice to follow.

Everyone heals differently. How your skin responds depends on the treatment, its strength, your skin type and how you care for it afterwards. The notes below describe what is common, not what is guaranteed. If anything worries you after a treatment, call the clinic on (555) 0148-2210.

Facials & chemical peels

Facials are usually gentle. Peels vary much more: a light peel may leave skin a little pink for a few hours, while a medium-depth peel is planned around several days of visible flaking.

What you may notice

  • Pinkness or warmth for a few hours
  • Tightness, then light flaking two to five days after a peel
  • Temporary sensitivity to products you normally use

Helpful afterwards

  • A gentle cleanser and plain moisturiser
  • Broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher every day
  • Letting flaking skin come away on its own

Best avoided for now

  • Exfoliants, retinoids and acids for about a week after a peel
  • Saunas, steam rooms and hard workouts for 24–48 hours
  • Picking or peeling loose skin

Microneedling

Skin usually looks flushed straight after microneedling, rather like mild sunburn. Many people plan the treatment ahead of a quieter day or two.

What you may notice

  • Redness for one to three days
  • A feeling of tightness or dryness
  • Fine, temporary roughness as skin renews

Helpful afterwards

  • The calming serum or balm we send you home with
  • Clean pillowcases and clean hands near your face
  • Daily SPF once the skin has settled

Best avoided for now

  • Make-up for 24 hours
  • Swimming pools and saunas for 48 hours
  • Active skincare until your practitioner says so

Injectables

After wrinkle-relaxing injections or dermal filler, small bumps at the injection points usually settle within hours. Bruising can happen and can take a week or so to fade.

What you may notice

  • Small raised marks that settle within hours
  • Possible bruising or tenderness
  • With filler, some swelling for a few days

Helpful afterwards

  • Keeping your head upright for four hours after wrinkle-relaxing injections
  • A cold compress, wrapped in cloth, for swelling
  • Coming to your two-week review

Best avoided for now

  • Rubbing or massaging the area for 24 hours
  • Strenuous exercise for the rest of the day
  • Facials or other treatments on the area for two weeks

Laser hair removal

Treated skin can feel warm and look slightly red or raised around each follicle, a little like goosebumps. Treated hairs may appear to grow for a week or two before shedding.

What you may notice

  • Warmth and redness for a few hours
  • Raised follicles that settle within a day
  • Shedding of treated hair over one to three weeks

Helpful afterwards

  • Cool compresses and a plain, fragrance-free moisturiser
  • SPF on any treated area exposed to sun
  • Shaving between sessions

Best avoided for now

  • Sun exposure and tanning before and after sessions
  • Waxing, plucking or threading during the course
  • Hot baths and saunas for 24 hours

Body treatments

Most body sessions are comfortable, but the area can feel warm or tender afterwards. Results, where they show, build gradually over weeks rather than days.

What you may notice

  • Warmth, mild redness or tenderness
  • Temporary numbness in some treatments
  • Gradual change over the course, which varies by person

Helpful afterwards

  • Drinking water and keeping active
  • Loose, comfortable clothing on the day
  • Attending review sessions so progress is measured

Best avoided for now

  • Very hot baths on the day of treatment
  • Expecting change after a single session
  • Booking other treatments on the same area without checking

Questions after a treatment?

Call or email the clinic during opening hours and a practitioner will call you back the same day. If you think you are having a serious reaction, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department.
