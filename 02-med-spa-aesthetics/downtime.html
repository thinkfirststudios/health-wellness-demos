Recovery

What each treatment really looks like afterwards

This page gives the recovery for every treatment we offer, in the same order as the treatment table. Results and recovery vary from person to person, and your own figures are confirmed at your consultation.

  • Medical director: a physician, on site every Tuesday and Thursday and on call on every other treatment day.
  • Practitioners: a nurse practitioner, registered nurses and a licensed aesthetician. Every current state license hangs at reception.
  • Who does what: set out against every treatment in the treatment table.

How to read the scale

The five steps describe how visible a treatment is at its worst. They are an order, not a length of time, and they are not a ranking of which treatment you should choose. The time figures sit underneath each scale.

  1. 1
    Seen up closeSomeone at conversation distance could notice.
  2. 2
    Seen across a tableNoticeable over coffee. Most people say nothing.
  3. 3
    Seen across a roomColleagues will see it, and some will ask.
  4. 4
    Hard to coverMakeup does not hide it. Many people stay home.
  5. 5
    Time away from peopleYou will not want to be seen. Plan around it.
