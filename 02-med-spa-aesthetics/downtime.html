How to read the scale

Each treatment has one scale of five steps. The filled span shows where that treatment sits; the step numbers are printed under every segment. The three figures beneath it are the ones people ask about: how long you will look visibly red or swollen, when you can go back to work, and when it has fully settled.

Every figure, step and sentence on this page comes from [MEDICAL DIRECTOR — CONFIRM], in writing, per treatment. Until then each one stays a visible blank.
