Treatments

Facials & Peels

Clinical facials are built around a skin assessment rather than a set menu: cleansing, gentle exfoliation, extractions where they help, and a mask matched to what your skin is doing that month. Chemical peels use measured acids to lift dull surface cells and are chosen by strength, from light lunchtime peels to deeper courses planned with your practitioner.

Learn more about Facials & Peels

Microneedling & Skin

Microneedling creates fine, controlled channels in the skin to support its own renewal, and is often planned as a short course for texture, enlarged pores or the look of acne marks. Your practitioner will talk you through numbing, how the skin looks in the days afterwards, and how many sessions are realistic for you.

Learn more about Microneedling & Skin

Injectables

Wrinkle-relaxing injections and dermal filler are prescription treatments, offered only after a medical consultation with a licensed nurse injector or physician. We favour a conservative approach, explain what each option can and cannot do, and schedule a review so any adjustment is planned rather than rushed.

Learn more about Injectables

Laser & Body

Laser hair removal is delivered as a course of sessions spaced weeks apart, after a patch test and a check of your skin type. Body treatments include contouring sessions and smoothing treatments for areas such as the thighs and upper arms, each started with a measured plan and honest expectations.

Learn more about Laser & Body

A clinic that starts with a conversation

Kilnwood is a small, physician-led aesthetics clinic. Nobody is booked straight into a treatment: you start with a consultation, where a licensed practitioner looks at your skin, asks about your goals and your history, and tells you plainly which options suit you and which do not.

Medical treatments are carried out by a registered nurse or physician. Facials, peels and laser are delivered by licensed aestheticians and certified laser technicians, working to protocols the medical director signs off.

Meet the team

The team

Roles, not names — every practitioner is licensed

Medical Director

A board-certified physician who oversees every medical protocol, signs off injectable plans and is on call during clinic hours.

Nurse Injector, RN

A registered nurse who carries out consultations and injectable treatments under the medical director's supervision.

Licensed Aesthetician

Leads facials, peels and microneedling, and writes the home-care plan you leave with.

Laser Technician

Certified in laser safety; carries out patch tests and laser hair removal courses.
