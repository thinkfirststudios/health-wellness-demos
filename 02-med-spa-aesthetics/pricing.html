Paying

Simple terms, written down

How to pay

Card, contactless and HSA/FSA cards where a treatment is eligible. Payment is taken at the end of each visit.

Cancellations

Please give 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations may be charged at 50% of the treatment price; we waive this for illness.

Price changes

Prices are reviewed once a year. A price quoted in your written plan is held for 90 days.
