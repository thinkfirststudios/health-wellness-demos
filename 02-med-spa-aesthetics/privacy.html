Privacy

What this site collects

Last updated November 6, 2026.

  • Medical director: a physician, on site every Tuesday and Thursday and on call on every other treatment day.
  • Practitioners: a nurse practitioner, registered nurses and a licensed aesthetician. Every current state license hangs at reception.
  • Who does what: set out against every treatment in the treatment table.

What we collect

Our forms ask for your name, a phone number or email address, your preferred contact method and one treatment category. The consultation form also records the time you chose. We use these only to reply to you.

What we ask you not to send

Please do not send health details, photographs or anything about a medical condition through this site or by email. We take those in person, at your appointment, into your patient record.

What this site does not do

  • It sets no cookies.
  • It runs no analytics, advertising pixels, session recording or chat tools.
  • It embeds no social media, maps or other third-party content.
  • Its fonts and images are served from this site, so your visit is not reported to anyone else.

Patient records

Once you become a patient, your record is handled under our separate notice of privacy practices, which we give you at your first appointment.

Membership billing

If you join the membership, we hold your payment details with our card processor to take the monthly payment. We never see or store your full card number.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you have sent us, email hello@kilnwoodclinic.example or call (603) 555-0142.
