Privacy notice

What this website collects and why

Sample policy for a demo website. A live clinic would have this reviewed by its own adviser.

Who we are

Kilnwood Aesthetics Clinic ("we", "the clinic") runs this website. You can reach us at hello@kilnwood.example, by phone on (555) 0148-2210, or by post at 214 Sample Street, Suite 3, Anytown, ST 00000.

What this website collects

Only what you type into a form: your name, email address, phone number if you give it, the treatment area you are interested in, your preferred times and any message. We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, chat widgets or social media plugins, and we do not set cookies.

What we do not ask for

The website forms do not ask for medical conditions, medications or a diagnosis, and we ask you not to include them. Health information is collected in person at your consultation and kept in your clinical record, which is handled separately under the clinic's patient privacy practices.

How we use your details

  • To reply to your message or arrange a consultation.
  • To keep a record of your request for up to 12 months.
  • We do not sell your details or use them for marketing unless you separately ask to hear from us.

Who we share it with

Our email and booking providers process messages for us under contract. We do not share form submissions with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details you sent through this website by emailing hello@kilnwood.example. We will reply within 30 days.

Under 18s

The clinic does not treat anyone under 18, and this website is not intended for them.

Changes

If we change this notice we will update it here. Last updated 4 November 2026.
