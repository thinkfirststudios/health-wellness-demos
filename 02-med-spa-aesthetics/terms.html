Terms of use

The small print, kept short

Sample terms for a demo website. A live clinic would have these reviewed by its own adviser.

This website

This site describes the treatments Kilnwood Aesthetics Clinic offers. It is general information, not medical advice, and it cannot tell you whether a treatment is right for you. Only a consultation with a licensed practitioner can do that.

Consultations

Every treatment follows a consultation. Your practitioner may advise against a treatment, suggest an alternative or ask you to wait, and may decline to treat if it would not be safe or appropriate.

Results

Results vary from person to person. We do not promise a particular outcome, and descriptions of recovery on this site describe what is common, not what will happen to you.

Prices

Prices on this site are a guide. The price for your treatment is confirmed in your written plan and held for 90 days. Packages are priced per visit; unused prepaid sessions are refundable at the per-visit price.

Cancellations

Please give 24 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment. Late cancellations and missed appointments may be charged at 50% of the treatment price, except for illness.

Reviews on this site

Reviews shown on this site are marked as samples and do not describe real clients.
