Facials & chemical peels

Our facials are clinical rather than purely relaxing, though most people still find them restful. Each one opens with a skin assessment so the products and techniques suit what your skin needs on the day.

Chemical peels are chosen by depth and by skin type. Light peels can fit into a lunch break; medium-depth peels need more preparation and more aftercare, and are only booked after a consultation.

  • Signature clinical facial$140 · 60 minDouble cleanse, skin analysis, exfoliation, extractions where needed, mask and protection.
  • Express facial$85 · 30 minA shorter cleanse, exfoliation and mask for skin that is already on a routine.
  • Light chemical peelfrom $165 · 45 minSurface-level acids for dullness and uneven tone. Mild flaking for a few days is common.
  • Medium-depth peelfrom $295 · 60 minA stronger peel for pigmentation or texture, with a pre-treatment skin plan and a follow-up check.

Microneedling & skin treatments

Microneedling uses a sterile, single-use cartridge of fine needles to create controlled channels in the skin. It is usually booked as a course of three to six sessions about four weeks apart.

We do not microneedle active acne, broken skin or recently tanned skin. Your practitioner will check this at the consultation and may suggest a peel or a facial first.

  • Microneedling, full face$325 · 75 minIncludes topical numbing, treatment and a calming mask. Redness typically settles over one to three days.
  • Microneedling, face and neck$395 · 90 minAs above, extended to the neck.
  • LED light add-on$45 · 20 minRed or blue LED after a facial or peel.
  • Dermaplaning$95 · 40 minGentle removal of fine surface hair and dead skin with a sterile blade.

Injectables

Wrinkle-relaxing injections and dermal filler are prescription-only medical treatments. At Kilnwood they are planned and carried out by a registered nurse injector or physician, after a medical consultation and a review of your history.

We take a conservative approach. If a treatment is unlikely to give you what you are hoping for, we will say so. A review appointment is booked two weeks after wrinkle-relaxing injections so any adjustment is planned.

  • Medical consultation$50, creditedCredited against treatment if you go ahead within 30 days.
  • Wrinkle-relaxing injectionsfrom $280 per areaCommonly the forehead, frown lines or crow's feet. Price confirmed at consultation.
  • Dermal fillerfrom $650 per syringeUsed to restore volume or soften folds. Amount and placement agreed in advance.
  • Review appointmentIncludedA two-week check after wrinkle-relaxing injections.

Laser hair removal

Laser hair removal works on hair in its growth phase, so it is planned as a course of sessions four to eight weeks apart. Most people need six or more; the number varies with hair colour, skin type and area.

Every new client has a patch test at least 48 hours before their first session. You will be asked to avoid sun exposure and to shave, not wax, between sessions.

  • Patch testFree · 15 minRequired before any laser treatment.
  • Small area$95 per sessionUpper lip, chin or underarms.
  • Medium area$165 per sessionBikini line, lower arms or lower back.
  • Large area$260 per sessionFull legs or full back.

Body treatments

Body treatments at Kilnwood are non-surgical sessions for areas such as the abdomen, thighs and upper arms. They are not a weight-loss treatment, and results differ between people; your practitioner will measure and photograph the area at the start, with your consent, so you can both review progress honestly.

We also offer back facials and smoothing treatments for areas that are hard to care for at home.

  • Back facial$120 · 50 minCleanse, exfoliation, extractions and mask for the back and shoulders.
  • Body smoothing treatment$150 · 60 minExfoliation and hydration for the upper arms, thighs or other areas.
