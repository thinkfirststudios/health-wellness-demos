Twelve treatments in four groups. Each row says who performs it, how long it takes, how visible the recovery is and what it costs.

  • Medical director: a physician, on site every Tuesday and Thursday and on call on every other treatment day.
  • Practitioners: a nurse practitioner, registered nurses and a licensed aesthetician. Every current state license hangs at reception.
  • Who does what: set out against every treatment in the treatment table.
Products and devices are described by what they are. Ask at your consultation and we will tell you exactly what we use and why. Results and recovery vary from person to person; every figure here is the range our medical director expects.

The four groups

Injectables

Prescription products given by injection. Every one of them needs a prescriber’s assessment first.

Minimum age 18. We do not see anyone younger, so there is no parental consent route.

Energy and laser

Regulated devices that deliver light or heat into the skin, each operated within the state’s rules.

Minimum age 18. We do not see anyone younger, so there is no parental consent route.

Skin

Peels and needling, from a lunch-hour visit to a week at home.

Minimum age 18. We do not see anyone younger, so there is no parental consent route.

Body

Treatments below the neck, done with the same recovery figures as everything else.

Minimum age 18. We do not see anyone younger, so there is no parental consent route.
