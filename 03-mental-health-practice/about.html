About the practice

Haverlock Therapy Group is on Pruett Street in Carrow Mills. We are a group of therapists with different licenses and ways of working, who share a building, an intake coordinator and a fee policy.

Our clinicians hold Pennsylvania licenses, and the license type differs by person. Each entry on Find a therapist shows that person’s license and the states where they can see virtual clients. Intake can give you any clinician’s license number on request.

How we work

Each clinician sets their own hours and caseload and describes their own work. The intake coordinator is the first person you speak to and handles scheduling, the sliding scale and referrals. The practice manager reviews fees once a year.

We publish our fees because you shouldn’t have to phone to find them out. We list everyone’s availability, including when someone has none, and the intake coordinator checks it every week.

Languages

Sessions are held in English. The therapists in Rooms 2 and 7 also work in Spanish, and the therapist in Room 8 also works in ASL.

Why there are no reviews here

We don’t publish client reviews, and we don’t ask for them. A client can’t freely say no to their own therapist, and being asked can change the work between you, so the ethics codes we practice under restrict it. If you want to know what someone is like, read what they’ve written about their work, or ask the intake coordinator.

Why we list rooms, not names

We’d rather you chose on what you can use: hours, cost, how someone works, and what they say about it. You’ll get your therapist’s full name and license number when your first appointment is confirmed.

Young people

We see young people from age 13. Who has to consent depends on the young person’s age, and intake goes through that with the family before the first appointment. Parents are told about attendance and about any safety concern. They aren’t told what is said in the room.

If you need help now

Call or text 988 at any hour. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. Haverlock doesn’t provide emergency or after-hours care. What to do if you need help now
