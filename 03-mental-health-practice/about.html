About us

A small practice with room to breathe

Haverlock started in 2012 with two therapists and a borrowed waiting room. We’ve grown, slowly and on purpose, into a team of six.

How we work

What we believe

Different therapists, different methods, a few things in common.

  • The fit matters most

    Research and experience agree: the relationship with your therapist matters more than the method. So we take matching seriously, and switching is always fine.

  • Plain language

    We explain what we’re doing and why. No jargon, no mystery, and no pressure to come more often than you need to.

  • Fees you can plan for

    Published prices, a real sliding scale and help with insurance, so cost is something you know before you start.

  • Ongoing training

    Every therapist has regular clinical supervision and keeps their training current, because good care takes practice.

Our rooms

Quiet rooms, soft light

Our rooms on Haverlock Street are on the second floor, with a lift, a step-free entrance and a waiting room that’s actually quiet. Each therapy room is soundproofed, with comfortable chairs and windows that open.

Our group room is larger and bright, and set up so everyone can see each other without feeling on display.
