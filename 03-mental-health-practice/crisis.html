If you need help now

Haverlock is not an emergency service. These lines answer at any hour.

  • Call 988

    The national Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. It answers at any hour.

  • Text 988

    The same service, by text message.

  • Call 911

    If you or someone else is in immediate danger.

What Haverlock can and can’t do

We don’t provide emergency or after-hours care. Nobody here answers the phone after 5 p.m. or on weekends, and voicemail isn’t checked until the next working day.

Out of hours, use the lines above. If you are a current client, you can leave your therapist a message and they will reply on their next working day.
