If you need help now

You don’t have to wait

Haverlock is not an emergency service and we can’t respond to urgent messages. These free services are available right now, 24 hours a day.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

If you’re in crisis or thinking about suicide, call or text 988. It’s free, confidential and open 24/7 across the US. You can also call if you’re worried about someone else.

Call 988Text 988

Emergency: 911

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, or has been seriously hurt, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department.

Call 911

Crisis Text Line

Prefer to text? Text HOME to 741741 to reach a trained crisis counselor, any time.

Text HOME to 741741

Veterans Crisis Line

Veterans, service members and their families can call 988 and press 1, or text 838255.

Call 988, then press 1Text 838255

If you’re already a Haverlock client

Your therapist will talk with you about a plan for difficult moments. Between sessions, please use the lines above if you need support urgently, and let your therapist know at your next appointment.

When things are less urgent

If you’re struggling but safe, you can request an appointment or call us on (555) 555-0142 during office hours. We reply within two working days.
