Insurance

We’re in network with several major PPO plans. We’ll check your benefits before your first session and tell you what you’re likely to pay.

Sample policy

Out of network

If we’re not in network with your plan, we provide a monthly superbill you can submit for reimbursement. HSA and FSA cards are accepted.

Sliding scale

Each therapist keeps a few reduced-fee places, from $80 a session. There’s a short, private form and no proof of income required.

Good to know

The small print, in plain words

Under the federal No Surprises Act, if you’re paying without insurance you have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate of expected costs. Ask us for one at any time.

How does cancellation work?

Please give us 24 hours’ notice. Late cancellations are charged at half the session fee. We waive this for illness and emergencies, no questions asked.

How do I pay?

By card, HSA or FSA after each session. Your card details are held by our payment provider, not on our systems.

Is the first call free?

Yes. The 15-minute call with our intake coordinator is free and there’s no obligation to book afterwards.

Do fees go up?

We review fees once a year and give existing clients at least 60 days’ notice of any change.

Can I use a sliding-scale place for couples or group sessions?

Yes, for couples sessions. Group fees are already set as low as we can keep them, so the sliding scale doesn’t apply to groups.
