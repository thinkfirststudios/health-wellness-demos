Fees, insurance and the sliding scale

What a session costs, which plans we take, and what to do if the fee is more than you can pay.

Our clinicians hold Pennsylvania licenses, and the license type differs by person. Each entry on Find a therapist shows that person’s license and the states where they can see virtual clients. Intake can give you any clinician’s license number on request.

The sliding scale

Is there a sliding scale?

Yes, for individual sessions with the clinicians marked in the list.

What is the range?

$70 to $160 for an ongoing session.

How do you ask for it?

Tell the intake coordinator, by phone or email, that you’d like the sliding scale. You don’t need to give a reason, and we don’t ask for pay stubs or tax forms.

Who decides?

The intake coordinator sets your fee on that call. Your therapist isn’t part of the conversation. You can ask to change it at any time.

Insurance

We are in network with Ardmoor Health, Penfield Mutual and Tessaly Care. Not every clinician takes every plan, so check the entry for the person you’re interested in.

With any other plan we are out of network. We give you a monthly superbill to send to your insurer.

We can’t tell you what your plan covers. Only your insurer can do that. Call the number on your card and ask about outpatient mental health benefits.

The superbill is free, and you can ask for one at any time.

Cancelling

Cancel or move an appointment with at least 24 hours’ notice and there is no charge. Inside 24 hours we charge the session fee, or your sliding scale fee if you have one. Insurers don’t pay for missed sessions. If something unavoidable happens, tell your therapist. They can waive the fee.

Paying

We take credit and debit cards, HSA and FSA cards, and checks. Payment is due at the time of the session. We keep a card on file only if you ask us to.
