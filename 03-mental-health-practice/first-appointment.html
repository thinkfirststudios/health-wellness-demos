The first appointment

What happens, what it costs, and what to do if it doesn’t suit you.

Our clinicians hold Pennsylvania licenses, and the license type differs by person. Each entry on Find a therapist shows that person’s license and the states where they can see virtual clients. Intake can give you any clinician’s license number on request.

What to expect

How long is it?

Sixty minutes. Ongoing sessions after that are fifty.

Who will you see?

The therapist you requested, on your own or with your partner if you’re coming as a couple. Nobody else is in the room.

What happens?

They’ll ask what made you get in touch now, what you’d like to be different, and a little about your life and health. You’ll be asked to read and sign a consent form beforehand, and they will go through what is confidential and what isn’t.

Will you have to tell the whole story on day one?

No. You can say as much or as little as you want, and you can say that you aren’t ready to talk about something.

What does it cost?

$190, or your plan’s amount if we’re in network, or the fee you agreed with the intake coordinator on the sliding scale.

What happens after?

Your therapist will tell you what they think and how often they’d suggest meeting. You don’t have to decide in the room. You can go home and call us.

What if it isn’t the right fit?

Say so, to them or to the intake coordinator. You can try someone else here or ask for names outside the practice. Nobody will ask you to justify it.

Before you come

What to bring

A photo ID, your insurance card if you’re using it, and the consent form if you filled it in at home. Nothing else.

Where to wait

The waiting room is on the first floor, past the front door on the left. There is also a quiet room, or you can wait in your car and we’ll call you. Your therapist will come and find you.

What if you need to cancel?

Call or email with at least 24 hours’ notice and there is no charge. Nobody will ask why, and you can rebook whenever you like.

And if you come by video?

You’ll get a link by email on the day. You must be physically in a state where your therapist is licensed, and you need somewhere private to sit.
