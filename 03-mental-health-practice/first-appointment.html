Your first appointment

What to expect

Knowing what will happen usually makes it easier to walk in, or log on. Here is the whole process, from first message to second session.

Step by step

Four small steps

  1. Get in touch

    Send a short request or call us. Tell us what kind of appointment you’re after and how you’d like us to reply. No need to explain everything yet.

  2. A free 15-minute call

    Our intake coordinator calls back within two working days to answer questions, check fees or insurance, and suggest a therapist who fits.

  3. Your first session

    Fifty minutes, in person or online. It’s mostly a conversation: what brings you here, what you’d like to be different, and whether this feels right.

  4. Carry on at your pace

    Most people meet weekly or every other week to begin with. You can pause, change therapist or stop at any point, no explanation needed.

On the day

It’s mostly a conversation

Your therapist will ask what brings you in, what’s been happening lately and what you’d like to be different. You can say as much or as little as you like. There are no wrong answers and nothing you have to prepare.

Towards the end you’ll talk about whether to meet again, how often, and anything that would make the sessions easier for you.

  • Arrive five minutes early if you’re coming in; there’s tea and a quiet waiting room
  • For online sessions, find a private spot and use headphones if you can
  • Bring your insurance card, if you’re using it

Questions

Things people ask us

What if I don’t click with my therapist?

Tell us, or tell them. It’s common and nobody will be offended. We’ll help you switch to someone else in the practice.

Is what I say confidential?

Yes, with a few legal exceptions, such as a serious risk to your safety or someone else’s. Your therapist will explain these in the first session.

How long does therapy take?

It varies. Some people come for six to eight sessions with a specific focus; others stay longer. You’ll review how it’s going together every few weeks.

Can I bring someone with me?

For individual sessions, you’re welcome to bring someone to the waiting room. If you’d like them in the session, mention it when we call.
