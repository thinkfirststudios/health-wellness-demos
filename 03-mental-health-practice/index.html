How it works

Starting is the gentle part

No forms about your history up front and no pressure to decide anything on the first call.

  1. Get in touch

    Send a short request or call us. Tell us what kind of appointment you’re after and how you’d like us to reply. No need to explain everything yet.

  2. A free 15-minute call

    Our intake coordinator calls back within two working days to answer questions, check fees or insurance, and suggest a therapist who fits.

  3. Your first session

    Fifty minutes, in person or online. It’s mostly a conversation: what brings you here, what you’d like to be different, and whether this feels right.

  4. Carry on at your pace

    Most people meet weekly or every other week to begin with. You can pause, change therapist or stop at any point, no explanation needed.
