Privacy

What this website does with your information. Effective October 1, 2026.

What this site collects

The only information this site collects is what you type into the appointment request form: your name, a phone number or email address, how you’d like us to reply, which clinician you’d like to see, in person or virtual, and a general time of day. The form has no space for anything else, on purpose.

The filter on Find a therapist runs in your browser. What you select isn’t saved or sent to us or to anyone.

What this site doesn’t do

This site has no analytics, no advertising or social media trackers, no chat widget, no embedded maps or videos, and no cookies. Its fonts and images are served from our own site, so a visit doesn’t tell any other company that you were here.

Who sees your request

The intake coordinator and the practice manager. It is held in our intake system under a signed business associate agreement, and we don’t sell or share it. If you don’t become a client, we delete it after 90 days. To have it deleted sooner, email or call us.

Notice of Privacy Practices

This page covers the website only. If you become a client, your health information is covered by our Notice of Privacy Practices, a separate document that explains how your records are used and what your rights are. You’ll receive a copy before your first appointment, and the intake coordinator will send you one at any time if you ask.

Questions

Write to the practice manager at 214 Pruett Street, Carrow Mills, Pennsylvania, or email intake@haverlocktherapy.com.

If you need help now

Call or text 988 at any hour. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. Haverlock doesn’t provide emergency or after-hours care. What to do if you need help now
