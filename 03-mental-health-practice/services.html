Services

Ways to work with us

Individual, couples and group therapy for adults aged 18 and over, in person or online. Every route starts with a free 15-minute call.

01

Individual therapy

50-minute sessions · weekly or fortnightlySample details

Most people come to individual therapy because something has started to feel heavier than it should. You don’t need a label for it. Your therapist will help you understand what’s happening, notice patterns and try things out between sessions, at a pace you agree together.

  • Anxiety, worry and overthinking
  • Low mood and loss of motivation
  • Burnout and work stress
  • Grief, loss and big life changes
  • Trauma, at a pace you set
Ask about individual therapy

02

Couples therapy

75-minute sessions · usually fortnightlySample details

Couples therapy gives you both a structured place to slow the conversation down. Your therapist stays neutral: the relationship is the client. Some couples come to reconnect, some to make a difficult decision well, and some to stop the same argument from running the house.

  • Communication that keeps going in circles
  • Rebuilding trust
  • Becoming parents, or deciding not to
  • Separating with care
  • Long relationships in a new chapter
Ask about couples therapy

03

Group therapy

90-minute sessions · six to eight peopleSample details

Our groups are small, closed once they start, and led by two therapists. Hearing your experience in someone else’s words can take a lot of the weight off. You choose how much to share, and there’s a short one-to-one conversation before you join so you know what to expect.

  • Burnout & boundaries (Thursdays, 8 weeks)
  • Men’s group (Tuesdays, ongoing)
  • Bereavement group (monthly, Saturdays)
  • Anxiety skills (Wednesdays, 6 weeks)
Ask about group therapy

04

Online therapy

Same therapists · secure videoSample details

Online sessions work the same way as being in the room. You’ll get a private link before each appointment; all you need is a quiet space, headphones and a steady connection. Many clients mix the two, coming in some weeks and joining from home others.

  • Secure, encrypted video platform
  • No app download needed
  • Available for individual and couples sessions
  • Ask about which states your therapist is licensed in
Ask about online therapy
