What we offer

What each service is for, who it suits and who it doesn’t. You can read these without deciding anything.

Our clinicians hold Pennsylvania licenses, and the license type differs by person. Each entry on Find a therapist shows that person’s license and the states where they can see virtual clients. Intake can give you any clinician’s license number on request.

Anxiety and worry

For worry that has started to take up your days: at work, at night, or around other people.

Who it’s for

Adults and teenagers whose worry gets in the way of work, sleep, or seeing people.

Who it isn’t for

Anyone who needs same-day or emergency support. See our crisis page.

Methods offered

Cognitive behavioral therapy, acceptance and commitment therapy, psychodynamic therapy

Who works with this

Room 1, Room 4, Room 5, Room 7. See them in the list →

Low mood

For a long flat stretch, losing interest in things, or finding the ordinary day harder than it was.

Who it’s for

Adults and teenagers who want to talk this through with one person, weekly or every two weeks.

Who it isn’t for

Anyone who needs medication managed. Our clinicians don't prescribe, and we can work alongside your doctor.

Methods offered

Cognitive behavioral therapy, psychodynamic therapy, acceptance and commitment therapy

Who works with this

Room 2, Room 3, Room 4, Room 5. See them in the list →

Grief and loss

For the death of someone close, and for other losses: a marriage, a job, a pregnancy, a parent's decline.

Who it’s for

Anyone over 18, however recent or long ago the loss was.

Who it isn’t for

Children under 13, whom we don't see. Intake can give you names of people who do.

Methods offered

Psychodynamic therapy, narrative therapy

Who works with this

Room 2, Room 6, Room 7. See them in the list →

Trauma

For the effects of something that happened to you, recently or a long time ago. You decide how much you talk about it, and when.

Who it’s for

Adults who are out of immediate danger and can come regularly.

Who it isn’t for

Anyone who is still in an unsafe situation. Please call us, and we'll talk through who can help first.

Methods offered

EMDR, cognitive processing therapy, psychodynamic therapy

Who works with this

Room 1, Room 6, Room 8. See them in the list →

Couples and relationships

For two people who want a third person in the room while they talk about something that keeps going wrong.

Who it’s for

Couples of any kind, married or not, together or deciding whether to stay together.

Who it isn’t for

Couples where one partner is afraid of the other. Please call and ask for an individual appointment instead.

Methods offered

Emotionally focused therapy, family systems therapy

Who works with this

Room 3, Room 8. See them in the list →

Teenagers, 13 to 17

For teenagers who would rather talk to someone outside the family, with a parent or guardian involved in ways agreed at the start.

Who it’s for

Young people aged 13 to 17. Who has to consent depends on the young person's age, and intake goes through that with the family first. Parents are told about attendance and about any safety concern. They aren't told what is said in the room.

Who it isn’t for

Anyone under 13, whom we don't see, and court-ordered assessments or custody questions, which we don't do.

Methods offered

Cognitive behavioral therapy, family systems therapy

Who works with this

Room 5. See them in the list →
