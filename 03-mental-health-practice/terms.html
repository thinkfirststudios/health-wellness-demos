Terms

The terms for using this website. Effective October 1, 2026.

What this site is

This site gives information about Haverlock Therapy Group, our clinicians, fees and building. Nothing on it is advice about your health, and reading it or sending a request doesn’t make you a client. You become a client when you and a clinician agree to work together and you sign our consent form.

Requests

The request form asks for an appointment. It doesn’t book one, and it isn’t monitored outside office hours. Please don’t use it if you need help urgently. Here is what to do instead.

Fees and availability

We keep fees and availability current, and each shows the date it was last checked. If something has changed, the fee or opening the intake coordinator confirms with you is the one that applies. We can’t tell you what your plan covers.

Virtual appointments

A clinician can only see you by video if you are physically in a state where they are licensed at the time of the session. Each clinician’s states are listed with their entry.

Changes

If we change these terms, we’ll update the date at the top of this page. These terms are governed by the laws of Pennsylvania.

If you need help now

Call or text 988 at any hour. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. Haverlock doesn’t provide emergency or after-hours care. What to do if you need help now
