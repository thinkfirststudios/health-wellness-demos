What happens in a session

If you’ve never done this, you probably can’t picture it. So here is each first appointment written down in order, from when you sit down to when you leave. Both run for 80 minutes, and both end with what doesn’t happen.

Acupuncture

first appointment 80 min · after that 60 min

  1. Minute 00

    You arrive and sit down in the room with your shoes on. Nothing happens to you for the first twenty minutes except a conversation: what you’ve tried, what you want to ask, what you’d rather not have done.

  2. Minute 20

    You lie down on the table. You keep your underwear on and roll your sleeves and pant legs up. There’s a sheet, and I step out while you settle.

  3. Minute 25

    Thirty to forty very fine needles, in your back, legs and hands. Most people feel a dull heaviness, then nothing. You lie still for twenty-five minutes, and I’m just outside the door if you call.

    If it is sharp, say so, and it is moved.

  4. Minute 55

    The needles come out. You sit up slowly. Some people feel light-headed for a minute or two; you stay until you don’t.

  5. Minute 75

    You can drive. You pay me before you go. I won’t call you afterward unless you ask me to.

What doesn’t happen

  • No diagnosis is made.
  • Nothing is sold to you.
  • No course of treatment is recommended.
  • I don’t comment on your medical care.

Breathwork

first appointment 80 min · after that 55 min, in a group of six or one-to-one

  1. Minute 00

    You sit with me, on a chair or a floor cushion, whichever you like. Comfortable clothes, shoes off. You talk through anything you’ve done like this before, which is often nothing.

  2. Minute 20

    I show you a pattern: in through the nose for a count, a short hold, out for longer. You try it sitting up, with your eyes open if you’d rather.

  3. Minute 35

    You lie down or stay in the chair and breathe to my counting for about twenty-five minutes. Some people feel tingling in the hands or a little light-headed.

    If it feels wrong, stop and breathe normally. Nobody will mind.

  4. Minute 60

    You sit up slowly and have some water. I write the pattern on a card and you go through it once more with me.

  5. Minute 75

    You leave with the card, so you can do it at home without me. You can drive. You pay me before you go.

What doesn’t happen

  • No diagnosis is made.
  • Nothing is sold to you.
  • No course of sessions is recommended.
  • I don’t comment on your medical care.
