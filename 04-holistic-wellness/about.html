Four people, four rooms, one front desk

Parson Street Wellness Rooms is four rooms above a shopfront. The four people who work here are each self-employed. They share the rooms, the front desk and this website.

Wren Adaba

Does the acupuncture

Works mostly with people who have never had it before, and with people who are nervous of needles. Explains everything before doing it.

Dessie Hallam

Does the massage

Deep, slow, firm work, and a lighter option for first-timers. A talker or a silent room, your call.

Fen Okoro

Holds the herbal and dietary consultations

A conversation about food, sleep and routine, with a written summary you keep.

Jules Markaryan

Teaches the breathwork

Groups of six, or one-to-one. Teaches it so you can do it without them.

How the front desk works

The desk is at the top of the stairs and it’s staffed whenever we’re open. It handles booking, payment, receipts and the phone for all four practitioners. If you call and nobody answers, you’re most likely hearing the desk’s voicemail during a busy moment, and we’ll call back the same day.

Who we see

People aged 16 and over. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian’s signed consent at the first appointment.

Languages

We speak English and Armenian. If you’d like an interpreter for another language, tell us when you book and we’ll arrange one.

The rooms

Each room has a door that closes, a lamp and a hook for your coat. Two have treatment tables, one has a table and two chairs, and one has floor cushions, chairs and space for a group of six.

Everything starts with the same 80 minutes.

$145, any of the four. Read what happens first, then book the one you want, or call and ask.
