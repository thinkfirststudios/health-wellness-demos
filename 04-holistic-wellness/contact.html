Getting here

My room is at 9 Parson Street, first floor: one flight of sixteen stairs up from the street, above the frame shop. The street door is the dark one to the left of the shop window, and it’s unlocked during opening hours.

The 14 bus stops on Parson Street, a two-minute walk from the door. Parking is in the Marl Street lot, two streets away, which has accessible spaces. There’s no parking on Parson Street itself.

There are sixteen stairs and no elevator. If stairs are a problem, read this first.

Everything starts with the same 80 minutes.
