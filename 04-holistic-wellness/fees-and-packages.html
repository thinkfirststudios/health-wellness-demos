Cancellation

  • Cancel or move an appointment up to 24 hours ahead and there is no charge.
  • With less than 24 hours’ notice, or if you don’t come, the charge is half the session fee.
  • If you’re ill, call me. There’s no charge for illness, however late it is.
  • If I have to cancel, you pay nothing and I rebook you first.

Why the first one costs more

It takes longer. It is a long conversation plus a short piece of the actual work, so you know what it feels like before you commit to anything. You don’t have to book a second session, and I won’t suggest that you should.

How payment works

You pay me before you go, by card or cash. Packages are paid for the same way. I don’t take a deposit and I don’t keep your card on file.

Receipts

Ask me or call me and I’ll give you a receipt for any session, at any time, with the date, the service, the fee and my name.
