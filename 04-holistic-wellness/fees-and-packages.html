Cancellation

  • Cancel or move an appointment up to 24 hours ahead and there is no charge.
  • With less than 24 hours’ notice, or if you don’t come, the charge is half the session fee.
  • If you’re ill, call us. There’s no charge for illness, however late it is.
  • If a practitioner cancels, you pay nothing and we rebook you first.

Why the first one costs more

It takes longer. It is a long conversation plus a short piece of the actual work, so you know what it feels like before you commit to anything. You don’t have to book a second session, and nobody will suggest that you should.

How payment works

You pay at the desk when you leave, by card or cash. Packages are paid for at the desk too. We don’t take a deposit and we don’t keep your card on file.

Receipts

Ask at the desk or call us and we’ll give you a receipt for any session, at any time, with the date, the service, the fee and the practitioner’s name.
