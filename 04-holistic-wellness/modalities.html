Four things, and how to tell them apart

What each one involves, how long it takes, what it costs, what to wear, and plainly who it’s not for. We describe what happens. We don’t say what any of it does for you, because that isn’t ours to say.

Acupuncture

80 min first · 60 min after that · $95

Very fine, single-use needles are placed in your back, legs and hands, and left in while you lie still. Wren explains each step before doing it, and most of the people who come here for it have never had it done.

  1. Minute 00

    You sit down, shoes on, and talk for twenty minutes.

  2. Minute 25

    Thirty to forty very fine needles. You lie still for twenty-five minutes.

  3. Minute 75

    You can drive. You pay at the desk.

What to wear

Wear something you can roll up to the knee and the elbow. You keep your underwear on.

Afterward

You can drive. Some people are light-headed for a minute or two before they leave, and we wait until that has passed.

Who it’s for

It’s for you if you’re willing to lie still while very fine needles are placed.

Who it’s not for

It’s not for you if you want to know what’s wrong: we don’t diagnose. It’s also not for you if you’d rather not have needles, and that’s a good reason to look at the other three.

Massage

80 min first · 50 or 80 min after that · from $90

Deep, slow, firm work by hand, with oil, on a table, under a sheet. There is a lighter option for first-timers, and you choose between a talker and a silent room.

  1. Minute 00

    You talk through where to work, how firm, and whether to talk at all.

  2. Minute 20

    Slow, firm work with oil, under a sheet. A lighter option if you ask.

  3. Minute 72

    You dress in your own time. You can drive.

What to wear

Whatever you like. You undress as far as you’re comfortable, in private, and you’re under a sheet.

Afterward

You’ll have oil on your skin and you’ll probably want some water. You can drive.

Who it’s for

It’s for you if you want slow, firm hands-on work, or the lighter version.

Who it’s not for

It’s not for you if you’re looking for an assessment or a course of sessions. Dessie doesn’t offer either.

Herbal and dietary consultation

80 min first · 45 min after that · $80

A conversation about food, sleep and routine at a table, with nobody touching you. It ends with a written summary you keep, and nothing is prescribed or sold.

  1. Minute 00

    You sit at a table with a pot of tea. Nobody touches you.

  2. Minute 05

    A long conversation about food, sleep and routine.

  3. Minute 75

    You leave with a written summary to keep.

What to wear

Whatever you’re wearing. You stay dressed and nobody touches you.

Afterward

You leave with a written summary of what you talked about. It’s yours to keep, and to show to anyone you like.

Who it’s for

It’s for you if you want a long, unhurried conversation about food, sleep and routine.

Who it’s not for

It’s not for you if you want a dietary plan, a prescription or a product. We don’t write plans, and nothing is prescribed, supplied or sold here.

Breathwork

80 min first · 55 min after that · from $30

A guided breathing practice, in a group of six or one-to-one. Jules teaches it so that you can do it at home without us.

  1. Minute 00

    You sit on a chair or a cushion and talk. Shoes off.

  2. Minute 35

    You breathe to Jules’s counting for about twenty-five minutes.

  3. Minute 75

    You leave with the pattern on a card.

What to wear

Comfortable clothes you can sit and lie down in. Shoes come off at the door.

Afterward

You leave with the pattern on a card. You can drive once any light-headedness has passed.

Who it’s for

It’s for you if you want to learn a breathing practice you can do at home.

Who it’s not for

It’s not for you if you’d rather have something done to you. This one you do yourself.
