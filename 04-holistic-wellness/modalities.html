Two things, and how to tell them apart

What each one involves, how long it takes, what it costs, what to wear, and plainly who it’s not for. I describe what happens. I don’t say what any of it does for you, because that isn’t mine to say.

Acupuncture

80 min first · 60 min after that · $95

Very fine, single-use needles are placed in your back, legs and hands, and left in while you lie still. I explain each step before I do it, and most of the people who come to me for this have never had it done.

  1. Minute 00

    You sit down, shoes on, and talk for twenty minutes.

  2. Minute 25

    Thirty to forty very fine needles. You lie still for twenty-five minutes.

  3. Minute 75

    You can drive. You pay me before you go.

What to wear

Wear something you can roll up to the knee and the elbow. You keep your underwear on.

Afterward

You can drive. Some people are light-headed for a minute or two before they leave, and I wait until that has passed.

Who it’s for

It’s for you if you’re willing to lie still while very fine needles are placed.

Who it’s not for

It’s not for you if you want to know what’s wrong: I don’t diagnose. It’s also not for you if you’d rather not have needles, and that’s a good reason to look at breathwork.

Breathwork

80 min first · 55 min after that · from $30

A guided breathing practice, in a group of six or one-to-one. I teach it so that you can do it at home without me.

  1. Minute 00

    You sit on a chair or a cushion and talk. Shoes off.

  2. Minute 35

    You breathe to my counting for about twenty-five minutes.

  3. Minute 75

    You leave with the pattern on a card.

What to wear

Comfortable clothes you can sit and lie down in. Shoes come off at the door.

Afterward

You leave with the pattern on a card. You can drive once any light-headedness has passed.

Who it’s for

It’s for you if you want to learn a breathing practice you can do at home.

Who it’s not for

It’s not for you if you’d rather have something done to you. This one you do yourself.
