Privacy

This site collects very little. Here’s all of it, in plain words.

Whose site this is

Parson Street Wellness Rooms is four self-employed practitioners sharing rooms and a front desk. The front desk runs this site and the booking form on behalf of all four. When you see a practitioner, what you tell them in the room is held by that practitioner, not by the website.

What the booking form collects

  • Your name
  • How you’d like to be contacted, and the number or email to use
  • Which of the four services you’re asking about
  • Whether this is a first appointment or you’re returning
  • What the message is about: booking, prices and packages, accessibility, or something else

There is no box for anything else, and that’s deliberate. Please don’t send us symptoms or medical history through this site. It isn’t a secure channel.

Who sees it

The front desk reads what you send and passes your name to the practitioner you’ve asked for. Nobody else sees it, and we don’t sell or share it.

How long we keep it

We delete booking messages after ninety days unless you’ve become a client, in which case your name and contact details stay in the appointment book.

What this site doesn’t do

  • No analytics and no advertising tags
  • No cookies
  • No chat widget and no session recording
  • No embedded maps, videos or social media
  • No requests to any other company’s servers: the fonts and photographs are stored on this site

Asking about your information

Call the front desk to ask what we hold about you, to change it or to have it deleted.
