Terms

The rules for booking, paying and cancelling, in plain words.

Who you’re dealing with

Me, Wren Adaba. I work alone and I handle the booking and the payment myself.

What this site is

This site describes what happens in the room. It doesn’t give health advice and it doesn’t replace or comment on your medical care. If you’re under a doctor’s care, tell them what you’re doing here.

Who I see

People aged 16 and over. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian’s signed consent at the first appointment.

Booking

Sending the form doesn’t book an appointment. You’re booked when I get back to you with a time.

Cancelling

  • Up to 24 hours ahead: no charge.
  • Less than 24 hours’ notice, or if you don’t come: half the session fee.
  • If you’re ill: no charge, however late it is.
  • If I have to cancel: you pay nothing and I rebook you first.

Paying

You pay me before you go, by card or cash. I don’t bill insurance, and I don’t keep your card on file.

Packages

  • Packages are prepaid and valid for twelve months from the day you buy them.
  • Unused sessions are refundable at the single-session rate. Used sessions are not.
  • A package can be transferred to someone else once.
  • Packages do not renew, and nothing is stored to charge you again.
  • There are no memberships or subscriptions.

Changing your mind in the room

You can stop any session at any time, for any reason, and you don’t need to explain.
